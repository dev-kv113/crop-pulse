Crop Analytics shows sugarcane farmers the boundaries of their own fields and the results of crop-condition surveys carried out by the mill. This policy explains what the app holds about you and why.

Last updated: [DATE]

Who is responsible

GEOTRANS TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED operates this app on behalf of [MILL NAME]. Contact: [EMAIL], [PHONE], [POSTAL ADDRESS].

What we collect

  • Your mobile number. Used only to sign you in and to identify which fields are yours. It is supplied by the mill from its own farmer records, or added by mill staff.
  • Your name and farm code, as recorded by the mill.
  • Your plot details — field boundaries, village, area, cane variety, survey dates and crop-condition results. This is the mill's survey data about land you farm.
  • Sign-in codes. Stored in a scrambled (hashed) form, valid for five minutes, and usable once.

What we do not collect

  • No password. Sign-in is by one-time code only.
  • No location tracking. The app never asks for your phone's location.
  • No contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone access.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking.

How it is used

Only to run the service: to sign you in, and to show you your own fields and their survey results. Your data is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Who else sees it

  • Mill staff. Unit officers can see the farmers in their unit and open the same screen a farmer sees. Mill administrators can see all units. This is how the mill supports its farmers.
  • Our SMS provider (MSG91). Receives your mobile number and the sign-in code, solely to deliver the message.
  • Map imagery providers (Esri, MapTiler). Your device fetches satellite tiles from them. They see the map area being viewed; they receive no information about who you are.
  • Nobody else. Other farmers cannot see your fields.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Data is stored on servers in [REGION, e.g. AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1)] and sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Sign-in codes are deleted or expired within minutes. Farmer and plot records are kept for as long as the mill maintains a relationship with you, and are removed on request as described below.

Deleting your data

You can ask for your account and personal data to be removed at any time. See Deleting your account for how, what is removed, and how long it takes.

Children

This app is for adult farmers and mill staff. It is not directed at children, and accounts are created by the mill rather than by self sign-up.
