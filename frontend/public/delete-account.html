Deleting your account

Farmers do not sign themselves up for Crop Analytics — the mill enrols you from its own farmer records. So there is no self-service delete button in the app. You can still have your account and personal data removed, and this page explains exactly how.

How to request deletion

Contact the mill by either route and say you want your Crop Analytics account removed:

Email
[EMAIL]
Phone
[PHONE]

Please include:

  • The mobile number you sign in with
  • Your name and, if you know it, your farm code

We may call the number back to confirm the request came from you, so that nobody else can have your account removed.

What happens

WhatResult
Your mobile number Deleted. You can no longer sign in.
Your name and farm code Deleted from the app.
Any sign-in codes Deleted. They expire within minutes anyway.
Field boundaries and survey results Kept by the mill as part of its own agricultural records, with your personal details removed. This is survey data about land, and the mill needs it to run its cane operations. If you want it removed as well, say so in your request and the mill will tell you what it can do.

How long it takes

Requests are actioned within 30 days. You will get a confirmation once it is done.

If you only want to stop using the app

You can simply uninstall it. Nothing is collected from your phone while it is not in use — the app has no background activity, and never accesses your location, contacts or files.
